# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder/Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors represent business leadership and strategic interests. They define business objectives, approve scope and budget, and serve as the primary escalation point for critical decisions and blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and strategic alignment
- Approve project scope, timeline, and budget
- Serve as escalation point for critical decisions and risks
- Ensure project outcomes align with organizational goals
- Communicate project status and value to senior leadership
- Provide stakeholder alignment and political support

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Minimize scope creep and timeline delays
- Enable timely decision-making through clear governance

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Receive escalations for decisions beyond project team authority; approve milestone gates
- **Product Managers**: Align on business priorities and success metrics
- **Developers**: Understand technical feasibility and constraints for business decisions

### Typical Communication
- Executive steering committee meetings
- Decision gates and approval sign-offs
- Business case reviews and ROI tracking

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads own quality standards, testing strategy, and risk mitigation. They ensure products meet acceptance criteria and quality benchmarks before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and quality standards
- Coordinate test planning and execution across sprints
- Identify quality risks and recommend mitigations
- Establish and maintain test automation and coverage targets
- Validate acceptance criteria are met before release
- Coordinate defect management and regression testing

### Goals
- Ensure product meets quality standards and user expectations
- Reduce defects in production through proactive testing
- Maintain high test coverage and automation levels
- Enable fast, confident releases

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Review test plans; collaborate on automation and code quality
- **Project Managers**: Report quality metrics and risks; influence release readiness
- **Product Managers**: Validate feature acceptance criteria; coordinate user acceptance testing

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and definition of done discussions
- Test reports and quality dashboards
- Risk assessment and release readiness reviews

---

## Technical Architect/Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects/Leads provide technical guidance, solution design, and technical risk assessment. They ensure architectural decisions support scalability, maintainability, and strategic technology goals.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and system architecture
- Provide technical guidance and mentoring to developers
- Assess technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Review code and architecture decisions for alignment with standards
- Evaluate technology choices and trade-offs
- Ensure adherence to technical best practices and patterns

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable systems
- Reduce technical debt and architectural risks
- Enable developer productivity through clear guidance
- Support strategic technology initiatives

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provide technical guidance; review designs and code
- **Project Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and timelines
- **Product Managers**: Influence technical feasibility of feature requests
- **Quality Assurance Lead**: Design testable architectures

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and technical mentoring
- Technology evaluation and RFC (Request for Comment) processes

---

## Operations/Support Representative

### Role Summary
Operations/Support Representatives bridge the gap between development and production support. They represent ongoing maintenance, operational concerns, and the voice of support teams in project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Represent production support and operational concerns
- Ensure operational readiness before releases
- Coordinate deployment and runbook creation
- Report operational issues and support burden impacts
- Contribute to incident response and post-mortems
- Ensure monitoring and alerting are in place

### Goals
- Ensure smooth production operations
- Reduce support burden and incident response time
- Enable reliable, maintainable services
- Improve operational visibility and response

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provide operational feedback; coordinate deployment and monitoring
- **Project Managers**: Advise on operational risk and readiness
- **Technical Architect**: Ensure designs support operational monitoring and scaling
- **Quality Assurance Lead**: Test operational scenarios and failure modes

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and runbook reviews
- Production incident reports and retrospectives
- Operational metrics and support dashboards

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers ensure adherence to security requirements, compliance standards, and risk management protocols. They protect organizational assets and ensure regulatory compliance.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and compliance standards
- Review designs and code for security vulnerabilities
- Manage risk assessments and compliance audits
- Coordinate security testing and penetration testing
- Ensure data protection and privacy standards
- Maintain security documentation and incident response plans

### Goals
- Protect organizational assets and customer data
- Maintain compliance with regulations and standards
- Reduce security risks and vulnerabilities
- Enable secure, trustworthy products

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Review security requirements; guide secure coding practices
- **Project Managers**: Communicate compliance requirements and timelines
- **Technical Architect**: Ensure secure architectural decisions
- **Quality Assurance Lead**: Coordinate security testing activities

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements and review meetings
- Vulnerability assessments and risk reports
- Compliance audit and certification updates

---

## Communications/Documentation Specialist

### Role Summary
Communications/Documentation Specialists manage internal and external communications, coordinate documentation updates, and ensure stakeholders are informed and aligned.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project documentation
- Coordinate internal communications and announcements
- Manage external stakeholder communications and briefings
- Create release notes and user-facing documentation
- Track decision logs and project artifacts
- Ensure clear, consistent communication across stakeholders

### Goals
- Maintain clarity and alignment across stakeholders
- Reduce communication gaps and misunderstandings
- Enable users to understand and adopt features
- Document institutional knowledge and decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate status updates and stakeholder communications
- **Product Managers**: Document feature requirements and user guides
- **Developers**: Translate technical details into user documentation
- **All Roles**: Capture and communicate key decisions and learnings

### Typical Communication
- Documentation reviews and updates
- Internal bulletins and announcement coordination
- Release note preparation and external communications

---

## Resource Manager

### Role Summary
Resource Managers allocate team capacity, manage skill gaps, and coordinate cross-team dependencies. They optimize resource utilization across projects and enable teams to deliver efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity and resource allocation
- Identify and address skill gaps
- Coordinate cross-team dependencies and handoffs
- Balance workload across multiple projects
- Facilitate resource escalations and conflicts
- Track resource utilization and capacity planning

### Goals
- Optimize resource utilization across projects
- Enable teams to focus on high-value work
- Reduce context-switching and dependency delays
- Support career growth and skill development

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Provide resource availability and capacity planning
- **Developers**: Assess skill needs and provide professional development
- **Technical Architect**: Identify specialized skills needed for technical initiatives
- **Executive Sponsor**: Report on resource constraints and trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Capacity planning and resource allocation meetings
- Cross-team dependency coordination
- Skill assessment and professional development discussions
